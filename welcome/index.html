What I’m pushing for

My Priorities

I’m one of five trustees. I can’t fix anything alone, and I won’t pretend I can.

  1. 01

    Keep Our Schools Open

    Closing a school can’t be undone. I’ll push for every alternative to be tried, measured, and published first, and I’ll call a closure a closure.

  2. 02

    Attendance and Enrollment First

    The deficit starts with declining enrollment, and the state funds us by days attended. Bringing families back and keeping kids in school protects classrooms, and that work should be community-led.

  3. 03

    Start at the Top

    A public look at district office spending before any cut reaches a school. Spending that starts at the top, not the classroom.

  4. 04

    Safe, Supported Students

    Protecting the supports that keep classrooms safe and teachable, like MTSS, and a real response to bullying in our middle and high schools.

  5. 05

    Options Families Choose

    ACCE hybrid and remote learning and independent study that can compete with any outside program, and outreach to homeschool families as partners.

  6. 06

    Fairer Funding

    Working with partners in Sacramento for funding that’s fairer to rural districts like ours.

My first 100 days.

My term begins in December. Here’s how I’ll spend the first 100 days, and what you’ll hear about along the way.

  1. Listen first

    I’ll visit every school in our area and hold open listening sessions for parents, teachers, and staff.

  2. Learn the numbers

    I’ll get fluent in the district budget, enrollment, and attendance, so I can explain where the money goes and why we’re in a deficit.

  3. Build working relationships

    One-on-one with each trustee, the superintendent, and our teachers’ and staff unions. We won’t agree on everything. We do all want our kids to succeed.

  4. Push for what we ran on

    Keeping our schools open, putting attendance and enrollment first, and taking a hard look at district office spending before anything touches a classroom.

  5. Report back

    Regular updates on what I’m learning and how I’m voting, so you never have to guess.

My word to you

How I’ll Serve

  1. 01

    Listen Before I Lead

    I’ll be in our schools, at listening sessions, and reachable. The parents and teachers closest to our kids know things no budget report will tell me.

  2. 02

    Bring the Numbers

    I’ve spent my career making data useful. I’ll ask clear questions, expect clear answers, and explain the budget in plain language.

  3. 03

    Disagree Respectfully

    I’ll work with every trustee and the superintendent, including when we see things differently. Strong boards argue about ideas and stay focused on kids.

  4. 04

    Be Transparent

    I’ll explain how I vote and why, and report back regularly so you never have to guess where I stand.
