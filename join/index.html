My why

I’m a dad, not a politician. Kids come first, and I’ll apply that test to every vote.

I ran because my kids go to Friendly Hills, and because I watched parents stand up at board meetings and town halls and feel unheard. I’m one of five trustees, and I can’t fix anything alone. Come along, and let’s build it together.

Thank you for trusting me. Now let’s get to work.

Building together

What We Care About

Closing schools is a failure of imagination, not the only path forward.

  1. 01

    Keep Every School Open

    I will not vote to close a Morongo school. Neighborhood schools are the heart of the Morongo Basin, and getting kids to school every day protects classrooms.

  2. 02

    Meet Families Where They Are

    ACCE hybrid and remote options and independent study should compete head-to-head with outside programs, and homeschool families should be partners, not written off.

  3. 03

    Spending That Starts at the Top

    Before any cut reaches a classroom, we should look hard at the district office. Our priorities have to be in balance.

  4. 04

    Strong Teaching for Every Child

    Every child deserves to learn and to feel safe and welcome. I’ll push to protect and rebuild MTSS and to support teachers so they don’t burn out.

  5. 05

    Use Our Schools Wisely

    Get La Contenta the help it needs now, develop a K–8 option, and redraw boundaries so every school carries a fair load and kids attend the school that fits them best.

  6. 06

    Families at the Table

    Parents should help shape school decisions, not hear about them after the fact. I’ll push for a Community Advocacy Council with a real role.

  7. 07

    Teachers Who Stay

    Good teachers should be able to afford to live where they teach. We have unused land, and we should make affordable housing for them.

  8. 08

    Technology That Serves Learning

    School devices should help kids learn, not feed them ads and algorithms.

  9. 09

    Bond Money You Can See

    Voters approved Measure C. Every dollar should be visible, and voter-approved work shouldn’t wait.

  10. 10

    Straight Talk

    You should never have to guess where the money goes or how I vote. That’s why you’ll hear from me regularly.
