Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 19, 2026

Juan Carlos Pineiro for Morongo Unified School District Trustee Area 5 2026 ("the Committee," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit withjuancarlos.com, complete a campaign form, volunteer, make a contribution through a linked service, sign up for text messages, or otherwise communicate with the Committee.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly, including:

  • Your name, email address, telephone number, and mailing address.
  • Volunteer interests, availability, and preferred method of contact.
  • Communications, questions, and other information you send to the Committee.
  • Text-message enrollment, consent, delivery, and opt-out records.
  • Contribution-related information required by campaign-finance laws.

Our website and service providers may also collect limited technical information, such as your browser type, device type, IP address, referring page, and general website activity.

Payment-card information submitted through a third-party contribution platform is processed by that provider. The Committee does not receive your complete payment-card number.

How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Respond to questions and requests.
  • Coordinate volunteers and campaign activities.
  • Provide campaign news, event information, voting reminders, and other requested communications.
  • Maintain records of text-message consent and opt-out requests.
  • Process and report contributions as required by law.
  • Operate, maintain, secure, and improve our website and campaign systems.
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, or security incidents.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, and campaign-finance obligations.

Text Messages

If you expressly enroll in the Committee's text-message program, we may send recurring campaign messages to the mobile number you provide.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel future messages. Reply HELP for assistance or email juancarlos@withjuancarlos.com.

Consent to receive text messages is voluntary and is not required to volunteer, donate, use the website, or otherwise support the Committee. We may retain records documenting consent and opt-out requests.

How We Share Information

We may disclose information:

  • To vendors that provide services to the Committee, such as website hosting, communications, contribution processing, data management, security, and compliance.
  • When required by campaign-finance, election, tax, court, or other applicable legal requirements.
  • When reasonably necessary to protect the Committee, website users, or others from fraud, misuse, security threats, or unlawful conduct.

We do not sell or rent personal information.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text-message opt-in information and consent records will not be shared with third parties, except service providers and telecommunications partners that use the information solely to operate the Committee's messaging program or satisfy legal and carrier requirements.

Campaign contributions and contributor information may be publicly disclosed when required by applicable campaign-finance law.

Third-Party Services and Links

Our website links to services operated by other organizations, including volunteer forms, contribution processors, and social-media platforms. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review those policies before providing information.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website or its service providers may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, understand general usage, maintain security, and improve performance. Your browser may allow you to limit or block cookies.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for campaign operations, regulatory compliance, dispute resolution, security, and recordkeeping. Legal obligations may require us to retain certain contribution, consent, or compliance records for a specified period.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect personal information. No website, database, or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

The website and text-message program are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

Your Choices

You may:

  • Opt out of campaign text messages by replying STOP.
  • Request text-message assistance by replying HELP.
  • Unsubscribe from campaign emails using the link provided in the email, when available.
  • Ask to review, correct, or delete information you previously provided, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
